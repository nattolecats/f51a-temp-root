--- related neverallow constraints ---

18700:(neverallow ephemeral_app gpu_device (chr_file (execute)))
19878:(neverallow isolated_app gpu_device (chr_file (ioctl read write getattr lock append map execute open watch watch_reads)))

Conclusion: base_typeattr_192 resolves to "appdomain and not isolated_app",
so every ordinary application domain, including untrusted_app (a third-party
app holding no Android permission), is allowed open/read/write/ioctl/map on
gpu_device, and /dev/kgsl-3d0 is mode 0666. The ioctls the PoC uses
(IOCTL_KGSL_GPUOBJ_ALLOC 0x45, IOCTL_KGSL_GPUOBJ_FREE 0x46,
IOCTL_KGSL_MAP_USER_MEM 0x15) are therefore reachable from an unprivileged
app, not only from the adb shell domain used during validation.
